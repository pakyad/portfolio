# Experience

## Part-Time Barista — Starbucks
**Company:** Starbucks
**Role:** Part-time Barista
**Dates:** November 2025 – August 2026
**Location:** Malaysia

Worked in a customer-facing environment while studying Software Engineering. The role involved working within a team, handling customers, maintaining service standards and operating during busy periods.

The experience gave exposure to a completely different kind of problem-solving from software development. Things move quickly, people communicate differently, and you often have to figure things out without having the luxury of sitting down and thinking about them for half an hour.

## Self-Employed Beverage Vendor

**Type:** Self-employed

What matters more is the pattern behind them: Iyad usually likes taking an idea from **concept → design → implementation → deployment**, rather than stopping at an isolated coding exercise.

He enjoys the whole process, including the parts where the original idea turns out to be weaker than expected and needs to change.

## What I Want in My Next Role
Established companies with experienced engineers, real systems, good mentorship, room to ask questions, some autonomy, real engineering work, people who care about doing things properly. An environment where disagreement or questioning isn't automatically seen as a problem.
