## What did formal education miss?
University provides the fundamentals, but independent projects forced the real decisions: architecture trade-offs, debugging production issues, choosing libraries vs building, and learning when "best practice" doesn't apply.

## Learning Approach
Iyad prefers documentation and seeing real implementations over generic programming books. He starts with AI explanations ("explain like I'm 5"), then tries the thing himself. If he's built with it, broken it, fixed it, and understood why it works — then it feels like his knowledge.
