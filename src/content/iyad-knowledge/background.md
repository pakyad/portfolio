## Academic Background Before Software Engineering
SPM in the Economics stream, with 7A's out of 8 subjects.

Before entering Software Engineering, Iyad studied Accounting at Kolej MARA Kuala Nerang (KMKN) through the Malaysian matriculation programme.

**Institution:** Kolej MARA Kuala Nerang
**Field:** Accounting
**Period:** Completed in May 2023
**CGPA:** 3.53

This was a deliberate change in direction rather than a continuation into an Accounting degree. After completing matriculation, Iyad decided that he wanted to move into technology and pursue Software Engineering instead.

The switch wasn't because Accounting was necessarily a bad fit. It was more that, over time, Iyad realised he was much more interested in **making things** than simply studying a field that seemed like the expected next step. Technology gave him the space to actually build, experiment and see an idea become something real.

## What sparked your interest in tech?
The exact story of what first sparked that interest is still something to refine. The important part is that the move into Software Engineering was a genuine pivot, not something he had planned from the beginning.

## Early interests that hinted at this path
Curiosity about how things work, taking things apart, and the satisfaction of seeing an idea become real. The pattern of noticing small problems people accept as normal and wondering "why are we all just accepting this?"

## What does "I like problems where the answer is a system" mean to you?
It means the interesting problems aren't solved by a single function or a quick fix. They're solved by understanding the constraints, designing a system that handles them, making deliberate trade-offs, and documenting why each decision was made. The portfolio itself demonstrates this — each project shows the constraint, the decision, the trace from problem to solution, and what broke along the way.
